# Lute 0.47.2 — decisions

Owner decision (2026-10-19): a patch release for two pre-existing player defects found while
implementing 0.47.1 and recorded in its decision note
([`2026-10-17-lute-0.47.1-decisions.md`](2026-10-17-lute-0.47.1-decisions.md), "Not fixed"),
filed as #137 and #138. Each issue body states the expected behaviour. The 0.47.0 **Testing
rule** applies: player / player-web behaviour is tested against real runtime output (a compiled
fixture through `@lute-lang/runtime`) or the player-web browser/e2e suite, never a hand-written
mock of a view or record.

| Issue | Decision |
|---|---|
| #137 (bug) | Every cue of a batch starts, whatever the autosave mode: the scheduler must not let the batch scope close before a forked non-waiting cue (camera, vfx, sfx) has started (`packages/player/src/scheduler/index.ts`). Regression test on a real compiled batch: a `::camera` and a `::sfx` as the last records before a `<choice>`, `autosave: false` and `"presentation"`, each effect observed. |
| #138 (bug) | A load completes while audio is still locked: restoring stage music never blocks the restore on playback start (`restartMusic`); music starts once audio unlocks. Browser test: load a save with stage music before any user gesture, reach the restored await, then a click starts the music. |

Out of scope: #139 (a literal `{` directly before a marker; contrived, low priority).

Version rule as 0.47.1: every visible axis aligns to `0.47.1` → `0.47.2`; schema file names stay
`-0.47`; `bindingsVersion` and the player save/profile format stay `"0.47"` unless a change
needs otherwise (record it here if so).

## Orchestrator choices (conservative, 2026-10-19)

| Topic | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Version contract | Patch on the 0.47 line, as 0.47.1: every visible axis stamps `0.47.2`; schema files keep `-0.47` names and byte-identical bodies; generated TypeScript unchanged; `bindingsVersion` and the save/profile format stay `"0.47"`. IR, Event and snapshot have no field or meaning change. | Neither fix needs a contract change; both are player TypeScript. |
| #137 "started" | `CueScheduler.play` completes only after every cue it forked (each non-waiting cue, each timeline clip) has started: delay (plus timeline `at`) elapsed, `CueStarted` published, `execute` begun running (skip: `complete` begun running); a fiber that exits before starting also releases the wait. A cue still in its delay when the mode becomes `skip` is completed at once. A delayed non-waiting cue holds the batch for its delay, not for its duration, in every autosave mode. Started one-shot cues are still not joined and are interrupted at batch end, as since 0.40 (0.40.0 §6.3 item 4 only says they are not joined). | "Every cue of a batch starts" covers delayed cues and the idle await (no autosave yields there in any mode); waiting for cues to finish would change when an await appears and is not asked. `startImmediately` alone would not cover a delay or timeline `at` (`raceFirst` already starts its racers at once in Effect 4.0.2). Skip races the delay: 0.40.0 §6.3 item 5, nothing waits on the clock in skip (ReviewSpec0472 M1). |
| #138 director | `presentStage` begins the music call (play or stop) on a fiber forked in the player (director) scope, started at once, and does not await it; the presenter restore is still awaited. Not interrupted by a later restore. The `AudioPort` docs state that a later music `play`/`stop` must win over an earlier music `play` still in progress. | "Never blocks the restore on playback start" for every `AudioPort`; interrupting an audio play mid-start can orphan a looping sound, so ordering is the port's (next row). Before, awaiting serialized the restored play before the next batch, so the ordering rule is now stated for custom ports (ReviewSpec0472 M3). |
| #138 player-web music order | In `PixiAudio` a music `play`/`stop` supersedes every earlier music `play` still in progress (waiting for unlock, its file, or the backend): the superseded play completes without starting — at once while it waits for the unlock, otherwise when its file load or backend play resolves; a backend play resolving after it was superseded is stopped at once and never recorded. Music already playing is replaced as before (crossfade, same-file volume change). sfx and voice unchanged. | Without it the mount's title music and the restored stage music both wait at the unlock gate and race when it opens; the decision's "music starts once audio unlocks" means the stage's music. A superseded play must complete, or a joined stage music cue (and every restore fiber) would wait forever (ReviewSpec0472 M2). |
